Curve

Press on the ball at address, slide until it sits under the magnifier, then release. Keep adding points along the flight, ending where it lands. Drag any point to adjust it. Three to five points is plenty.

To find the ball, pinch to zoom or scroll the mouse wheel. Move around with two fingers, or switch on the hand button to drag with one.

Slowdown

Step to the frame of impact and press Launch. Step to where the ball comes down and press Landing. The tracer runs between them, quick off the club and slowing towards the end.

Each point is pinned to the frame it was placed on, and the tracer reaches it on that frame. Select a point, step to the frame where the ball is really there, and press Pin.

The snake thins and fades towards its tail. Set the length to 100% to keep the whole line on screen. After landing, the tail slides on to the landing point and the line disappears.

Export

Export records the clip in real time, so a 10 second clip takes about 10 seconds. Keep this screen open while it runs.

Saving is not available in this view. Open the page in the Claude app or on claude.ai, or press and hold the preview above.

Everything runs in your browser. The tracer is plotted by hand, so it shows the line you draw and does not measure distance, speed or spin.
