New points go

The first point is the Start and the last is the End. Choose “After selected” to put a new point between two others, and use the Move buttons to reorder. Or press Arc to have one smooth flight drawn between Start and End.

Curve

Press on the ball at address, slide until it sits under the magnifier, then release. Step forward a few frames and tap the ball again, ending where it lands. Each point is pinned to the frame it was placed on, so the line follows the real ball. Drag any point to adjust it.

To find the ball, pinch to zoom or scroll the mouse wheel. Move around with two fingers, or switch on the hand button to drag with one.

Perspective

Optionally step to where the ball is highest and press Ball at the top, so the tracer reaches the top on that frame. Where the tracer is working the flight out for itself, it shoots up off the club and slows as the ball gets further away, the way it looks on camera. Slide Perspective down for a more even speed.

Each point is pinned to the frame it was placed on, and the tracer reaches it on that frame. Select a point, step to the frame where the ball is really there, and press Pin.

Line width

The snake thins and fades towards its tail. Set the length to 100% to keep the whole line on screen. After landing, the tail slides on to the landing point and the line disappears.

Export

Output

For Final Cut, iMovie, CapCut and similar. You get a video of just the tracer on black. Put it on a track above your clip, lined up at the start, then set its blend mode to Screen (Final Cut: Video inspector, Compositing, Blend Mode). The black disappears and only the tracer shows, while you keep full control of your own edit. Whole video is selected so it lines up with your clip.

Export records the clip in real time, so a 10 second clip takes about 10 seconds. Keep this screen open while it runs.

Saving is not available in this view. Open the page in the Claude app or on claude.ai, or press and hold the preview above.

Flightline is part of Beyond the Shot. Everything runs in your browser. The tracer is plotted by hand, so it shows the line you draw and does not measure distance, speed or spin.
